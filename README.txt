Custom HTML/CSS/JavaScript website. Open index.html, or upload index.html, styles.css, script.js and assets/ to your web host. Typography: Inter loads from Google Fonts. Headings specify Avantt with Inter fallback. Supply your Avantt webfont files to embed it for all visitors; no Avantt files were provided. Card track is full bleed; header and desktop copy retain 150px padding. Hover swaps filled/outline buttons and reveals a black-to-transparent card gradient.

SEO: Title, meta description, canonical, Open Graph/Twitter metadata, JSON-LD, robots.txt and sitemap.xml are included. Production URL is https://mydetailiq.com/. Upload robots.txt and sitemap.xml at the domain root along with the site. If deploying to a different domain, update canonical, og:url, JSON-LD URLs, robots.txt and sitemap.xml. This owner-private preview is not publicly crawlable. Submit the production sitemap in Google Search Console after deployment. Internal sample links still need their destination pages.
