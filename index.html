FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

WHAT IS DETAILIQ?

DetailIQ is scheduling, invoicing and customer software for auto, marine, RV, motorcycle, pressure washing, fleet and aircraft detailers. It runs your job calendar, recurring plans, customer portal and invoicing, with one flat price per plan instead of a fee per seat. DetailIQ is a PayWavez company.

DO I NEED A CREDIT CARD TO START?

No. Sign up and start using DetailIQ without a card. Your free period runs through November 30, 2026. After it ends you have 7 days to add a card; without one, your account turns read-only and your booking page stops taking bookings until a card is added. Nothing is deleted. Accounts created after November 30 get the same 7 days from signup.

WHICH PAYMENT PROCESSOR DOES DETAILIQ USE?

Card-on-file and invoice payments run through your own PayWavez merchant account, so the money lands in your account, not ours. No separate gateway to wire up.

CAN I CHANGE PLANS LATER?

Yes. You can move between Starter, Pro and Max as your team grows. There is no long-term contract.

WHICH KINDS OF DETAILING DOES DETAILIQ SUPPORT?

Auto detailing, marine and boat detailing, RV and motorhome, motorcycle, pressure washing, fleet and truck washing, and aircraft detailing. Vehicles get VIN decoding and a service history; boats, RVs and properties get their own asset records.

IS DETAILIQ AN ESTABLISHED COMPANY?

DetailIQ is a young product, and we are upfront about that. It is built by the team behind PayWavez and its sister product Pandarly, and it runs on the same PayWavez payment infrastructure and support team. Founding Member pricing exists because early customers help shape the product.
